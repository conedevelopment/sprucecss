Opt-in text utilities ('utilities': ('text': true)) and the always available accessibility helpers.

Text alignment

.text-start — aligned to the start.

.text-center — centered.

.text-end — aligned to the end.

Text styles

.text-sm — a smaller text.

.text-lg — a larger text.

.text-muted — a secondary, muted text.

.text-mono — monospace text.

.text-uppercase — uppercase text.

Text wrapping

.text-balance — a long heading that is balanced over multiple lines for nicer reading

.text-pretty — avoids orphans at the end of paragraphs. Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. Phasellus vel tortor mi, vivamus bibendum erat lacus.

.text-truncate — Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. Phasellus vel tortor mi, vivamus bibendum erat lacus, quis tincidunt urna dictum non.

.text-nowrap — this text never wraps.
